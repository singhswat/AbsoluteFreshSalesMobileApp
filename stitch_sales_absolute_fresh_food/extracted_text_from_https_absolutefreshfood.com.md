### We show you _every_ ingredient.  
Because there are only five.

01

Idli Rice

The Foundation

Short-grain rice chosen for idlis — soaks right, grinds right, gives that cloud-soft bite every time.

02

Urad Dal

Black Lentil · The Fluff

Split black gram gives the batter its rise and the spongy texture that separates great idlis from average ones.

03

Chana Dal

Bengal Gram · The Body

A small addition that adds subtle depth and helps the batter hold its shape beautifully when poured.

04

Poha

Flattened Rice · The Softness

Beaten rice blends into the batter for a lighter, more delicate texture — the secret to crispy dosas.

05

Methi Seeds

Fenugreek · The Character

Kickstarts fermentation and adds that unmistakable slightly-earthy note of authentic South Indian batter.
